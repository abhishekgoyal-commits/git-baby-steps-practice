# How to Fill Out the Status Report

Use this document to complete the status report template consistently.

## 1. Summary
- Project/Team: Name the project or team being reported.
- Reporting period: State the date range or sprint window.
- Overall status: Choose one of Green, Yellow, or Red.
  - Green: on track
  - Yellow: some risk or delay, but manageable
  - Red: material risk or missed commitments
- Executive summary: Provide a short paragraph summarizing overall progress.

## 2. Key Updates
- Completed this period: List the major work completed since the last report.
- In progress: List the planned or active work still underway.
- Risks/blockers: Record issues that could affect delivery, including dependencies or impediments.
- Decisions needed: Note any approvals, escalations, or stakeholder decisions required.

## 3. Metrics
- Sprint / milestone progress: Describe current progress against sprint or milestone goals.
- Delivery vs plan: Compare actual progress to the expected plan.
- Team capacity / workload: Include workload trends, staffing changes, or overload concerns.
- Quality / defects: Mention defect counts, quality issues, or rework if relevant.

## 4. Next Steps
- Immediate actions: List actions needed in the next few days.
- Dependencies: Mention teams, tools, or external inputs needed.
- Planned milestones: Note upcoming dates, deliverables, or checkpoints.

## 5. Notes
- Additional context: Include any relevant background, assumptions, or changes since the last report.
- Stakeholder concerns: Capture concerns raised by clients, leadership, or other stakeholders.

## Tips
- Keep the executive summary brief and actionable.
- Be honest about risks and blockers.
- Use consistent status terms across reports.
- If a metric is not available, record that explicitly rather than guessing.
